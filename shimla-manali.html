Trip Itinerary Summary

Day 1: Arrival & Drive to Shimla

On arrival at Delhi Airport/Railway Station, you will be met, assisted, and driven to Shimla—the former summer capital of British India.

Day 2: Kufri Excursion & Shimla Local Sightseeing

Post breakfast, proceed for an excursion to the green valleys of Kufri (Winter Sports Capital, Himalayan Zoo). Afternoon visit Christ Church, Jakhu Temple (via ropeway), Gaiety Theatre, Scandal Point, Lakkar Bazaar, and Mall Road.

Day 3: Shimla to Manali via Kullu Valley

Morning drive from Shimla to Manali (approx. 8–9 hours journey). En-route pass through Pandoh Dam, Beas River, and Kullu Valley.

Day 4: Solang Valley Excursion

Proceed for a day excursion to Solang Valley—a splendid valley offering breathtaking views of glaciers and snow-capped peaks.

Day 5: Manali Local Sightseeing

Visit Vashisht Hot Water Springs and Temple, Hidimba Temple, Tibetan Monastery, Club House, Van Vihar, and Manali Mall Road.

Day 6: Departure

Check out from Manali hotel and proceed back to Chandigarh/Delhi with fond memories.
