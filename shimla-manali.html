Suggested itinerary

Your 6-day Shimla-Manali journey

This is a sample route. Final sightseeing and travel times may vary by pickup point, road conditions, weather and local restrictions.

DAY 01

Arrival & transfer to Shimla

Meet at the agreed pickup point and drive towards Shimla. Check in to the hotel and relax, depending on arrival time.

DAY 02

Kufri & Shimla sightseeing

Explore Kufri and selected Shimla attractions such as Christ Church, Mall Road and Lakkar Bazaar as time permits.

DAY 03

Shimla to Manali via Kullu Valley

Travel through mountain scenery towards Manali. This is a long road journey, so sightseeing stops depend on timing and conditions.

DAY 04

Solang Valley excursion

Visit Solang Valley if accessible. Adventure activities, snow and other experiences depend on season, weather and local availability.

DAY 05

Manali local sightseeing

Visit selected attractions such as Hadimba Temple, Vashisht, a Tibetan monastery, Van Vihar and Mall Road.

DAY 06

Departure

Check out and transfer to your agreed drop point. Please allow sufficient time for the return road journey.
